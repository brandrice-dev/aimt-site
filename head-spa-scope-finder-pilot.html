Don't guess what your license covers.

The phrase “head spa” doesn't tell you whether your license permits each step. Start with the specific services you want to offer, find your state's official guidance, and take unanswered questions to the licensing authority.

What this tool can and cannot do: It can point you to official sources and organize a precise licensing-board question. It cannot clear you to perform a treatment, interpret ambiguous law, confirm business permits or replace qualified regulatory advice. Only five states currently have curated links; all other states are intentionally marked unmapped.
