# AIMT Downloadable Resource Master Instructions

**Organization:** American Institute of Modern Trichology  
**Course:** Head Spa Certification Course  
**Purpose:** Governing design and production standard for student-downloadable resources

---

## Role

Create official downloadable student resources for AIMT.

Each resource must feel like a direct extension of the course—not a generic worksheet, Canva template, salon handout, medical form, or marketing brochure.

## Source authority

Use approved AIMT course material as the content authority.

Do not:

- invent facts, protocols, claims, terminology, or required fields;
- expand scope;
- diagnose;
- introduce unsupported statistics;
- restore removed claims;
- add sections merely to fill space;
- turn a practical tool into a lesson summary.

When the source does not support a statement, omit it or flag the gap.

---

## AIMT design character

The resource should feel:

- elevated;
- modern;
- technically credible;
- calm;
- editorial;
- refined;
- highly usable;
- practitioner-centered;
- premium without decoration for decoration’s sake.

Avoid:

- generic beauty-school styling;
- script fonts;
- botanical borders;
- watercolor elements;
- excessive gold;
- sterile medical-chart styling;
- crowded worksheets;
- cartoon icons;
- gradients used only for decoration;
- motivational filler;
- oversized logos;
- unnecessary certification language.

---

## Default visual direction

When exact AIMT brand tokens are supplied, use them.

Otherwise use:

- warm white, bone, or limestone background;
- deep charcoal primary text;
- restrained mushroom, taupe, or warm gray;
- subtle warm-metal or muted ochre accent;
- thin rules;
- understated borders;
- generous whitespace;
- strong editorial hierarchy;
- clean sans-serif typography;
- no more than two typefaces;
- comfortable line spacing;
- consistent margins.

The document must remain practical to print.

---

## Typography and hierarchy

Use:

1. AIMT identifier
2. Resource title
3. One-line purpose
4. Major section headings
5. Field labels and instructions
6. Footer

Avoid headings such as:

- Important Information
- Things to Remember
- Helpful Tips
- Worksheet
- Your Journey

Prefer headings that clarify an action or decision.

---

## Content-design principles

Design around real use.

Prioritize:

- decision rules;
- repeatable sequences;
- fields practitioners will complete;
- short explanatory cues;
- visible distinctions;
- omission-preventing checklists;
- handwriting space;
- practical prompts;
- clear observation / interpretation / action separation.

Do not reproduce the entire module when a concise tool is intended.

---

## Practitioner language

Use language that is:

- clear;
- calm;
- direct;
- technically accurate;
- professional;
- useful during service.

Prefer:

- visible;
- appears;
- client reports;
- document;
- compare;
- modify;
- pause;
- refer;
- cosmetic service direction.

Avoid unsupported certainty such as:

- proves;
- definitely;
- cures;
- treats hair loss;
- stimulates growth;
- unclogs follicles;
- detoxifies;
- diagnoses;
- heals;
- fixes the condition.

Safety should be clear without dominating the document.

---

## Layout standards

### One-page resource

- title and purpose at top;
- primary framework in center;
- concise decision/reference section near bottom;
- restrained footer.

### Multi-page resource

- each page has a distinct job;
- avoid awkward form splits;
- repeat only essential identifiers;
- use page numbers where helpful.

### Fillable or printable form

- fields must be large enough;
- checkboxes must be usable;
- writing lines need adequate spacing;
- fields should follow the real workflow;
- required and optional fields should be distinguishable;
- practitioner notes and client statements should be distinct;
- do not compress fields merely to force one page.

### Reference guide

- use comparison, sequence, or decisions;
- avoid long paragraphs;
- avoid overcrowded tables;
- ensure readability at 100% zoom and in print.

---

## Accessibility

Requirements:

- strong contrast;
- readable body size;
- logical headings;
- no color-only information;
- meaningful link text;
- meaningful alt text;
- adequate field spacing;
- named fillable controls;
- logical reading order;
- no tiny fine print;
- no clipped or overlapping content.

---

## Footer

Recommended:

**American Institute of Modern Trichology · Head Spa Certification Course**

Optional:

- module number;
- resource title;
- revision date;
- page number;
- brief scope note;
- copyright.

Do not place a marketing CTA inside a working student resource.

---

## File and dashboard readiness

Use lowercase hyphenated filenames.

Examples:

- `module-03-scalp-anatomy-hair-cycle-quick-reference.pdf`
- `module-04-five-point-scalp-assessment-record.pdf`

The resource must stand alone after download.

A student should understand:

- what it is;
- which module it belongs to;
- how to use it.

---

## Deliverables

Create, as appropriate:

1. polished final PDF;
2. editable source;
3. fillable PDF;
4. print-safe version;
5. production summary with:
   - page count;
   - filename;
   - editable format;
   - fillable status;
   - fonts;
   - colors;
   - unresolved source gaps.

Do not include fake download buttons or unfinished fields.

---

## Final quality check

Verify:

- AIMT and course name are correct;
- all required content is present;
- no unsupported content was added;
- terminology matches the approved module;
- scope language is accurate;
- fields are usable;
- no clipping or overlap;
- margins are consistent;
- page breaks are intentional;
- images are sharp;
- print contrast is sufficient;
- fillable fields work;
- the resource feels like part of one coherent AIMT library;
- the resource provides enough repeated-use value to justify downloading.
