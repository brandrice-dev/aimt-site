# Before You Buy — AIMT Head Spa Equipment Clinic
**Status:** Presenter/production brief; no event scheduled, promoted or recorded
**Format:** One pilot live clinic, 45–60 minutes (including audience Q&A), plus replay and checklists
**Education goal:** Help a prospective practitioner avoid preventable equipment and installation mistakes, while showing AIMT's real operating experience and decision-making model.
**Owner's direct experience (known):** has used both plumbed and non-plumbed beds, replaced bed parts, and modified non-plumbed setups with larger water tanks and a tank water heater; has evaluated purchasing through U.S. vendors versus direct sourcing from China. **No specific failure, cost, vendor allegation, or successful modification outcome is assumed** until captured firsthand.

## 1. Public registration promise (draft)
**Before You Buy: Head Spa Equipment Clinic**

*Buying a head spa bed? Get the questions right before you spend.*

A practical, live conversation about plumbed and non-plumbed head spa beds, supplier differences, repairs, replacement parts, and the setup questions that can delay an opening. Bring your questions. Leave with a more useful equipment-buying checklist.

**Free · Live Q&A · Includes a checklist**

No earnings claim, medical promise, approved equipment list, or implied guaranteed compatibility. "Ask your local licensed installer, authority and insurer" for project-specific compliance questions. We do not recommend unsafe retrofits or unapproved electrical/plumbing modifications.

## 3. Founder interview to capture authentic teaching material
Record 15–25 minutes of unscripted audio first. Editor asks, one topic at a time:

### Operating the systems
1. What was the original reason you selected a plumbed or non-plumbed setup?
2. What does an ordinary treatment day reveal about each system?
3. How much additional labor did filling, draining or sanitizing actually create?
4. What would surprise someone who has only watched social media demonstrations?
5. What determines whether water capacity is enough **for your setup**? (No universal tank-size recommendation.)
6. What would you ask an installer before bringing equipment into a suite or existing salon?

### Repairs and modifications
7. Which parts did you replace most often? Which were difficult to source?
8. What was the first problem that made you rethink the original setup?
9. What did you learn from expanding tanks or changing hot-water capacity?
10. Which repairs were routine maintenance, and which needed a qualified professional?
11. What safety, sanitation or code questions would you insist on checking before attempting any change?
12. If you bought again, which spare parts / manuals / support contacts would you verify first?

### Buying in the United States vs importing
13. Which purchase routes have you personally used? What documentation can be shown?
14. What were the **actual total costs**, including freight, taxes/duties where applicable, installation, service and downtime? Verify before publishing numbers.
15. How did supplier communication and warranty fulfillment differ?
16. Were parts identifiable and interchangeable, or proprietary?
17. What questions would you send to both vendors before committing to a purchase?
18. Did anything challenge your assumptions about more expensive or less expensive options?

### Education / judgment
19. What's one expensive mistake someone could prevent with a 10-minute research step?
20. What is the first decision you would help a new graduate make? How would that differ for an established salon owner?
21. When is "wait and verify the space" better advice than "buy now"?
22. What is one piece of advice you're *not* comfortable generalizing from your own experience?

**Editorial standard:** Separate (a) direct verified experience, (b) professional opinion, (c) applicable product instructions and code, and (d) unresolved vendor/building-dependent questions.

## 4. Equipment buying checklist — attendees leave with these questions
### Space and building
- Do we have the actual room dimensions, ingress and access clearance?
- Who confirms the fixture's load, power supply and local plumbing/electrical requirements?
- Who confirms water supply, hot water capacity, water pressure and discharge path?
- Can a licensed installer review the manufacturer's actual specification before payment?
- Are tenant/landlord, inspection, permit, insurance and establishment requirements known?

### Equipment and sanitation
- Is the system plumbed, portable or an attachment? Which exact model is it?
- Is the flow fresh water or recirculated, and how does the manufacturer require cleaning?
- How much filling, draining, disassembly and between-client sanitation time does it need?
- What is included/excluded (halo, steamer, controls, heaters, pumps, hoses, traps)?
- Can I obtain replacement parts with actual part numbers and clear service instructions?
- Is the maker's maintenance process appropriate for the intended client volume?

### Seller and after-sales
- Is the seller the manufacturer, authorized distributor, importer or unrelated reseller?
- What is the **written** warranty and who pays shipping/labor if a part fails?
- What is the return policy if the bed arrives damaged, incomplete or incompatible?
- Are replacement parts stocked, and where/how do I order them?
- What are the invoice, manual, serial/model identification, safety certifications, installation requirements and shipping terms?
- What are the *landed* and installed costs, not only the product listing price?
- How are delays, damage claims and ongoing technical support handled?
- What documentation should be inspected before any direct import or utility connection?

**Do not instruct attendees to install/mod equipment themselves.** In-session advice should be framed as decisions and verification questions.
