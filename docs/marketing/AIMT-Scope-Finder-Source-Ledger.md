## Tool version 1
The first useful version should do three things, in order:
1. Ask for state and professional license type.
2. Show the **actual licensing-board primary source** and the exact activities the user needs to verify (shampooing/cleansing hair, cosmetic scalp care, massage/bodywork, blow-drying/styling, cameras/consultation, facility/equipment/sanitation).
3. Generate a copyable, narrow inquiry the practitioner can send to the board, with the source/date and unanswered questions.

Never interpret `head spa` as a single statutory license category, or equate an AIMT certificate with state permission to practice. No "clearance" or green lights. If a source does not clearly settle an activity for a particular credential and facility, show **Unresolved — ask your board**, even if an adjacent state allows it.

## Unanswered questions in any jurisdiction
- Does the professional license allow *each* step proposed (cosmetic scalp work, shampoo/cleanse of hair, massage of specific body regions, use of devices, drying/styling)?
- Does the service require a licensed facility/establishment, additional permit, inspection or supervision?
- Does the local board distinguish hair cleansing from scalp cleansing?
- Are there local public-health, sanitation or waste-water requirements?
- Does the professional's insurance cover the proposed service and equipment?
- Which board governs a technique that crosses another profession's scope?

## Copyable neutral board request
> Subject: Clarification of permitted activities under my [LICENSE] in [STATE]
>
> Hello, I hold a current [LICENSE] in [STATE]. I am considering a cosmetic scalp-care/head spa service at [GENERAL FACILITY TYPE]. Before offering any treatment, I want to verify the rules for these *specific* activities: [ACTIVITY LIST].
>
> Could you identify which activities fall within my license's legal scope, whether another credential or establishment permit is required, any relevant sanitation or equipment requirements, and the statute/regulation or published guidance supporting your answer?
>
> Thank you.

Do not include license number, client details or identifying documents inside AIMT planning tools. A copy button can copy this neutral request; the student sends it using their own email client. Do not connect a public form to the licensing board on their behalf.
