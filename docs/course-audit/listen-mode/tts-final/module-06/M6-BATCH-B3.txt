Section 6.7 — Treatment within scope. You are supporting the environment. Not treating the condition.

Cleansing support: regular cleansing reduces excess oil, buildup, and the microbial overgrowth that Malassezia feeds on. Consistency matters more than intensity.

Anti-fungal supporting ingredients: zinc pyrithione, 1%-strength ketoconazole, and 1% selenium sulfide are OTC categories that help manage yeast activity. Never recommend or name a prescription-strength, 2%, product.

Anti-inflammatory botanicals: licorice root and chamomile calm the scalp — reducing redness, irritation, and discomfort without disrupting the barrier. Preferred over aggressive treatments in a head spa context.

And regular maintenance: irregular care leads to fluctuation in symptoms. The most effective approach is consistent support over time — not aggressive intervention when things flare.

[warmly] A product category is not a diagnosis. Recommending one is a retail suggestion within a cosmetic service — never a diagnosis, never a prescription. If a presentation meets any of the referral criteria we just covered, refer instead of recommending a product.
