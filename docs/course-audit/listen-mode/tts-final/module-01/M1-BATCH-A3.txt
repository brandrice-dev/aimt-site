Before your first checkpoint, a quick practice round — ungraded. Where is the line? Read each statement and decide for yourself whether it's professional observation or outside scope.

Statement one: "I'm seeing redness in this area. I would avoid aggressive exfoliation here and explain when medical evaluation would be appropriate." ... Correct answer: professional observation. This describes what is visible, adjusts the cosmetic service, and recognizes when referral may be appropriate without naming a condition.

Statement two: "This is seborrheic dermatitis. You should use a medicated shampoo twice a week." ... Correct answer: outside scope. This names a diagnosis and directs treatment. A head spa technician may describe visible findings and recommend medical evaluation, but should not diagnose or prescribe.

Statement three: "I can document the flaking I see, adjust today's service, and explain that appearance alone cannot determine the cause." ... Correct answer: professional observation. This keeps the practitioner grounded in observation and service adjustment without presenting a medical conclusion.

Statement four: "Your follicles are clogged, and that is why your hair is thinning." ... Correct answer: outside scope. This presents an unverified cause as fact. The practitioner may document visible buildup or thinning, but cannot determine the medical cause from appearance alone.
