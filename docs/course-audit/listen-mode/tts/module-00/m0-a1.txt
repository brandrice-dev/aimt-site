Section 0.2 — What this course is. A framework. Not a script.

This is a complete service framework. It teaches you to think through the head spa experience as a practitioner — not to copy movements or memorize a sequence. You'll learn service structure, client flow, assessment thinking, scalp pattern recognition, treatment logic, setup, positioning, and sanitation.

And just as important — you'll learn how to stay in your lane. You are not being trained to diagnose or treat medical conditions. You're being trained to observe, recognize patterns, educate appropriately, and know when not to proceed. That distinction matters, all the way through this course.

Two things worth stating precisely here. What this course is not: this is not the only way to perform a head spa. Rigid systems create fragile practitioners. The goal is understanding, not imitation — so you can adapt based on your space, your tools, your client, and your scope.

And what your certificate represents: completing AIMT's Head Spa Certification Course means you've worked through the required curriculum and demonstrated the course competencies. Your certificate documents that achievement. It is not a state license, a medical credential, or permission to work beyond what your existing license and local laws allow.
