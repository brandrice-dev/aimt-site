The AIMT Model

Not more content. Better preparation.

Built from practice
Education should survive contact with a real client, a real schedule, and a real business.

AIMT programs are designed around professional decisions, practical constraints, and the places where practitioners commonly lose confidence after the textbook answer runs out.

Built for modern learning
